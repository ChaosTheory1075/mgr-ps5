PPSA48080/install/ - put your Metal Gear Rising: Revengeance files here
======================================================================

- The game (required): your disc image (.iso, any name), or a folder of the
  disc's files (default.xex and the rest).
- Title Update 4 (required): its package file, tu00000001_00000000 (any
  name). Other title updates can't be used: the app runs Title Update 4's
  code.
- DLC (optional): the packages from 4B4E080A/00000002/ (names like
  4C526F5275130ABB7264B6E8FDFD1AC461C2EBFC4B), as many as you have.
- Saves (optional): an Xbox 360 save package, copied off the 360's drive
  (Content/<profile>/4B4E080A/00000001/<save>). Import it from the SAVES page.

The files have no extension; that's normal. The app tells what each one is
from what's in it, whatever it's called. Extract any .7z, .rar or .zip on a PC
first.

Open the app: it installs the game, the title update and the DLC by itself,
one at a time, then asks whether to delete the files it installed from
(X: delete, O: keep; kept files aren't installed again).
